# Applekin (Common), Growth Stage 1 — generated references

Six transparent PNG concepts based on the tiny, apple-free tree in the top row of the user-supplied approved Applekin design sheet.

The filenames describe the requested views: front (0°), back (180°), left (90°), right (270°), front three-quarter (45°), and back three-quarter (135°).

These are independently generated images, not renders of one shared 3D model. Geometry, leaf placement, framing, and actual camera orientation are not consistent or verified across views. They are visual concepts, not an exact modeling turnaround. No 3D model is included.

The generator returned 1254 × 1254 RGBA PNGs despite a 1024 × 1024 request. Originals are preserved here. A shared 3D model and six camera renders at 1024 × 1024 are still needed to meet the original exact-turnaround specification.
